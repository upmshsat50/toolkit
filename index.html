Community as classroom

Health professions education rooted in the realities of the Filipino community.

The School of Health Sciences was established around a distinctive social mission: to develop health professionals who are clinically competent, socially accountable, and committed to underserved and marginalized communities.

Community-based learning allows students to understand disease in context—families, livelihoods, local governance, health programs, referral systems, geography, and the social conditions that shape health.

01

Social accountability

Training is directed toward the health needs of communities, especially those historically underserved.

02

Primary care

Students learn to work within local health systems, RHUs, barangays, and referral networks.

03

Continuity

Community work gains value when assessment, interventions, partnerships, and lessons continue across batches.

The SHS tradition

The step-ladder model connects education, service, and community need.

UPM-SHS traces its roots to a health manpower development model created to respond to maldistribution of health workers and the needs of rural communities.

Community Start with the health needs of people and place.
Competence Build progressive health-profession skills through real service settings.
Return service Develop practitioners committed to serve where they are most needed.
Leadership Strengthen local health systems through socially accountable practice.

Community medicine learning pathway

From entering a community to leaving a useful handover.

The toolkit follows the actual work of community clerkship and internship rather than functioning as a separate administrative system.

01

Prepare

Orientation, scope, referral pathways, community history, and prior project review.

02

Enter

Build relationships with local leaders, RHUs, barangays, and community partners.

03

Assess

Understand health needs using community data, local knowledge, and health-system realities.

04

Plan & Act

Develop feasible interventions with shared priorities and clear local ownership.

05

Monitor

Track outputs, outcomes, lessons, limitations, sustainability, and next steps.

06

Hand over

Leave the next batch with usable records so meaningful work can continue.
