About the program

Community medicine as a living learning environment.

Community-based medical education moves learning beyond the classroom and hospital. Students engage with local health systems, community priorities, health programs, families, and the everyday conditions that shape access to care.

The digital Community Health Toolkit is envisioned as a companion to this training—helping preserve institutional memory, improve project continuity, and make essential resources easier to access across student batches.

Learning pathway

From community entry to continuity.

The toolkit is organized around the actual flow of community-based medical learning.

01

Prepare

Orientation, site familiarization, scope of practice, stakeholders, and referral pathways.

02

Understand

Community profile, health-system mapping, local priorities, and previous batch records.

03

Assess

Community diagnosis, family and population perspectives, and data-informed priority setting.

04

Act

Planning, implementation, stakeholder coordination, and community-engaged interventions.

05

Monitor

Indicators, evaluation, documentation, reflection, and sustainability planning.

06

Hand over

Structured endorsement so the next batch can continue meaningful work instead of starting over.
